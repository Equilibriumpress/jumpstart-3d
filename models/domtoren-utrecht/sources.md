# Domtoren Utrecht — source register (PR 1)

The model is an **architectural study**, not a measured survey. Primary height data are from the City of Utrecht; plan widths are taken from a published scholarly description. Drawings inform further detailing, but no unverified measurement should be presented as surveyed.

## Published numerical sources

1. Gemeente Utrecht, *Domtoren*, https://erfgoed.utrecht.nl/verhalen/domtoren — height 112.32 m, first square 38.60 m, second square 29.48 m, octagonal lantern 26.00 m, top of spire 106.75 m. Primary reference for vertical datum.
2. E. J. Haslinghuis & C. J. A. C. Peeters, *De Dom van Utrecht*, 'B. De toren', published via DBNL, https://www.dbnl.org/tekst/hasl001domv01_01/hasl001domv01_01_0006.php — lower square 19.30 m wide, second square 16.34 m wide, octagon approximately 13.50 m wide, lower arch passage approximately 4.13 m wide and apex 10.25 m above ground; note historical total height 112.50 m differs from modern municipality figure. Also places octagon beginning at approximately 69 m while the two square heights add to 68.08 m: include an explicit transition of 0.92 m, not a false claim of an exact floor-level boundary.

## Modeling limitations and quality gates

- Widths do not establish the exact depth, orientation or window layout. Use square-plan assumptions only for PR 2 massing and mark them illustrative.
- A pointed arch needs an authentic profile, not a square hole painted black. PR 2 creates a true pass-through with structural posts and a pointed recess silhouette. PR 3 should use measured 2D tracery profiles to revise it.
- The octagon must be genuinely open between eight pillars; a solid drum with painted windows is insufficient.
- Do not count the 112.50 m historical height as the current published 112.32 m.
- Compare front, side and 3/4 views against post-restoration photographs before claiming geometric accuracy.

## Production

Canonical scale 1:500; full scale metres to model millimetres = 2.0. Coordinates: x east-west, y north-south, z above street grade. All recipe data measured in model millimetres.
