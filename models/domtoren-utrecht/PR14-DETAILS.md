# PR 14 — ornament and materials

The existing four clock plates now include editable annular stone rims, 12 hour markers, distinct hour/minute hands and a central boss. The first and second promenades have independent parapet rails and 72 stone balusters. Added 64 shallow stone-course lines, 8 roof ribs and 8 gallery corner pinnacles.

All surfaces remain flat-colored native CAD bodies. The colours are a restrained visual palette, **not** photographic weathered stone or PBR texture. The clock times, ornamental dimensions and individual masonry courses are aesthetic study choices, not archival survey facts. Source assets remain licence-free procedural geometry.

The height and the open gallery are preserved. Live OpenCascade/Pages rendering must still be reviewed before treating the model as visually verified.
